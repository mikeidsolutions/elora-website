01 · The idea

What Elora means

Elora draws on Hebrew and Greek roots — “God is my light” and “sun-ray / light.” The brand carries that meaning as a feeling, never a statement: light, growth, and steady ground for people managing their mental health.

Hope & strength

Every choice — a rising line, an even weight, a warm tone — should feel like steady footing and a way forward. Calm, never clinical-cold; hopeful, never saccharine.

Light, not religion

The practice works from a faith-based model, expressed only as light and growth. Nothing overtly religious, nothing decorative or floral.

Growth of the mind

Themes of the brain healing and growing — the sun-ray and plant-vine motifs — stand in for progress and recovery over time.

Androgynous & warm

Clean, minimal lines with a human warmth. Deliberately not feminine, not sterile — welcoming to everyone.

02 · Voice

How Elora sounds

Warm, steady, and plainspoken. We talk with people, not at them.

“Your first session is a conversation, not an assessment.”

Principles

Speak to “you”; we are “we.” Verbs over nouns — “Book a session,” not “Session booking.” No urgency, no jargon, no fear.

Mechanics

Sentence case everywhere, including buttons and headings. No emoji. Short sentences. Plain words a first-time patient understands.

05 · Wordmark

The wordmark

Until the final mark is in place, Elora is set as a lowercase Cormorant Garamond wordmark. Lowercase keeps it approachable; the serif keeps it warm and grounded.

elora
Do
  • Keep clear space of one “e”-height on all sides.
  • Use pine on light, isabelline on pine.
  • Keep it lowercase.
Don't
  • Stretch, tilt, or add effects.
  • Recolor outside the palette.
  • Set it in another typeface.

A custom mark (sun / light + brain-growth themes) is being developed separately by the client — this wordmark is the interim lockup and will pair with that mark once finalized.
