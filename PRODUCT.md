# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Children (ages 12+), adolescents, and adults managing anxiety, depression, ADHD, trauma, or sleep issues who are considering psychiatric care. Many are arriving anxious about the process itself, possibly booking their first-ever psychiatric appointment. There is no secondary audience; the site speaks directly to the person who will book their own care, not to family researching on their behalf or to referring providers.

## Product Purpose

Elora is a psychiatric practice built around hope and steady ground. The site exists to get a visitor comfortable enough to book a first session — treating that first session as a conversation, not a clinical assessment. Success is booked first sessions through the primary CTA.

## Positioning

Care that moves at the patient's pace, not a fixed treatment timeline. Where other practices lock patients into a rigid schedule toward a diagnosis, Elora adjusts session by session — steady, unhurried, with no fixed endpoint.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Book a session. No secondary fallback CTA — the site stays single-minded around booking.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "Your first session is a conversation, not an assessment."
- Belief ladder: (1) this is a conversation, not an intake checklist — Book; (2) a plan gets built around your actual life, not just a diagnosis — Build; (3) care continues at your pace with no fixed timeline — Grow. This ladder is already expressed in the site's "How it works" section.
- Proof on hand: provider credentials — Suzanne Perakathu, APRN, PMHNP-BC, board-certified with over 8 years of clinical experience — now anchor the site via a dedicated provider bio section. Testimonials, licensing detail, and insurance-partner logos remain a gap to fill later.

## Brand Personality

Hopeful, steady, and warm. Elora's name draws on "light" and "sun-ray" roots, carried as a feeling rather than a statement — never overtly religious, never decorative or floral. The tone is calm and grounded, never clinical-cold; hopeful, never saccharine. Visually and verbally androgynous: clean and minimal with human warmth, deliberately not feminine-coded or sterile, welcoming without assumptions about who's arriving. Voice is warm, steady, plainspoken — speaks to "you," never issues checklists, avoids urgency and jargon.

## Anti-references

No specific competitor sites named as anti-references; the existing brand guardrails are considered sufficient: nothing overtly religious, nothing decorative or floral, nothing feminine-coded, nothing that reads as cold/clinical/institutional.

## Design Principles

- Conversation before assessment — every surface should feel like being met in conversation, not processed through a form or checklist.
- Steady pace over urgency — no countdown timers, no scarcity language, no rushed motion; the interface itself should never hurry the reader.
- Welcoming without assumptions — nothing in layout, imagery, or copy should presume a visitor's gender, background, or diagnosis.
- Light and growth as feeling, not statement — sun-ray and growth motifs stand in for hope and progress; they're never explained or made overtly symbolic/religious.
- Single-minded toward booking — one primary action (Book a session), no competing secondary CTAs diluting the ask.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline. Motion stays calm and settling (existing `prefers-reduced-motion` support should be preserved in all future work); avoid anything that could read as urgent or overwhelming to a visitor already under stress.
